## Supported-ground finish handoff

A physically qualified actual standing arrival enters phase33 before passive
coast rejection. It retains the current approach and issues bounded four-tick
**literal manual native input**, not a direct position write or an air-guide
correction. Entry retains the conservative inset landing predicate. Walking can
use the raw region or body overlap with the captured doorway, but must still have
actual grounding and flat static-world support at the captured floor height.

Straight steering was insufficient: an acting run retained the landing and
executed240manual ticks, then paused at the doorway's concrete side post with no
backtracking. The body's measured position and offline BSP collision plane agree.
The portal itself starts at floor level; a raised-portal/jump hypothesis was not
assumed or implemented. This failed finish is retained, not called a pass.

The corrected local route actually finishes through the portal with0backtracks
and a complete strict0fault REC14 (952inputs,4boards). The normal production arm
remains12.285/4backs,819inputs,6boards/strict0faults; it does not activate this
ground tail, so the separately acting midpoint-seeded arm is essential. This
improves keeping a feasible arrival, not necessarily its race time:14.280 is slower
than the old midpoint's eventually selected13.110. Do not call this a universal
speedup or compare failed direct-walk pauses to a completed run.

The local route first tries a full-hull clear path into the portal. Otherwise
it chooses a supported face approach whose current->approach and approach->portal
static traces are clear. Before consuming a nearby approach waypoint it rechecks
**actual-current-position->portal** visibility; distance alone cannot cut a
corner. No clear route pauses without ramp backtracking. This one-waypoint
visibility route is not a general multi-corner/obstacle navigator.

Ownership, geometry and actual support are rechecked before each bounded grant.
A two-game-second no-progress watchdog uses the latched `run_t_tick`, not fixed

platform checkpoint. Other maps, moving support, narrow doors and changed-world
