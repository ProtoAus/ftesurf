# P828 reader rollout — 2026-10-19 UTC

## Delivered change and limits

A complete `holdclock 1` recording may thaw after its last input row. Its closing
`inend` must reach that last hold observation in the **current mover origin**.
P828 rejects a closing raw counter before that boundary. Equality/later closes,
origin resets, live prefixes and unsupported capability abstention are preserved.

This is raw chronology, **not** proof of the timer's initial origin or an equality
between elapsed trailer ticks and mover span. It changes no recording format,
native code, thresholds, permissions or ranking policy. No calibration, hardware
attestation or automatic historical receipt reread is claimed.
