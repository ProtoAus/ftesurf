  space, no longer switch mouse ownership; the wheel over the panel scrolls two
  list rows per step. Other panels and text-field held-key ownership stay ahead

  `ent_view_dist`. Turning off the viewer clears its publications. Explicit
  `r_showbrushes` settings take precedence and are never rewritten. Collision,
  movement, timer/evidence and backend behavior are unchanged.
