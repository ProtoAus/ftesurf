## P1387: Patch 860's spinners, sliders and their props

- Server (sv_entities.qc): SV_SpResolve makes every needed mover a CSQC entity (ENTWIRE_SPSTATE 7,
  SV_SpSend) unless its chain has a solid member or an ambient_generic sounds from a travelling member
  (`vbsp_sp_keep` on the root: the whole chain stays server-written as before). Sent: kind, model, parent
  (entity), spawn-frame pose (lorg/lang), axis, movedistance/startposition, look; then the clock (rot:
  th0/t0/w0/wt/up/dn/stopping/stopat; lin: pos/ideal/dir/speed/t0), again on any SpinInput or START_ON
  use; any other input resends the look. Its edict is never written. Sent by PVS: each chain member gets
  one box round its root, as far as the chain reaches (`vbsp_sp_reach`), so a rider is sent with its
  parent; a P1202 sky mark on any member sends the chain always. SV_SpFrame wakes 20 Hz only while a
  server-written mover moves; otherwise only for events (START_ON, arrival outputs, the 61 s re-anchor).
- Kill of a parent: SV_SpKillKids places each child now and leaves it parentless where it is, its own
  turn or slide continuing (a slide's axis taken into the world frame). Going solid (SV_SpDrop): the whole
  chain returns to ordinary networking (SV_SpUncl from the root; size and pvsflags restored), the driver
  wakes, and a drawn mover's riders stay where they are.
- Shared math (sh_spin.qc Sp_Theta / Sp_LinPos) is what SV_SpRotTheta / SV_SpLinPos now call.
- Client (cl_spin.qc): Sp_Place composes the chain per frame (cached per time, Trk_Ride's composition),
  re-anchors past 60 s as the server does; links by a box any turn stays inside.
- Behaviour change: a tools-only mover P860 left unwritten ("draws nothing") is drawn turning, as in
  Source; before, the engine drew it at its spawn pose.
- Census of the arm maps (`spin: ...` line): surf_centipedes 27 of 27 drawn by csprogs, bhop_lost_world_v2
  96/96, surf_voyager 71/74, surf_santorini 4/4, surf_gigapede 1/7 (solid in the other chains).

## P1388: path_track speeds

- sh_trk.qc: the path table keeps each node's `speed`; a train's trk_vmode is 1 (each node's speed on
  arrival: No User Control, spawnflag 2), 2 (velocitytype 1, linear blend toward the next node's speed by
  distance) or 3 (velocitytype 2, ease in/out as four linear pieces), 0 when no node on its path has one
  or neither applies. Find's ArriveAtNode gives a No User Control train its first node's speed at spawn.
- Trk_S steps a per-train checkpoint over segments (closed-form time/distance for speed linear in
  distance; Trk_Ln is ours: builtin #532 is MVDSV's precache_vwep_model on the server); a loop is timed
  over one steady lap (second to third arrival at its first node), arrivals snapped to that lap grid and
  whole laps skipped from then; cap K*(6n+16) steps. Backward motion keeps one speed (not modelled).
  SV_TrkInput re-anchors at the profile's current speed, only for inputs it handles.
- Library (tools/trkspeeds.py over Momentum's maps): 32 passable trains have node speeds; they apply to
  christmas2's 12 (linear), bhop_collective crt3-6 (instant) and trike_t (ease), bhop_alt_spacement papa,
  bhop_furret train2, bhop_minecraft_spiral vagon/vagon2, bhop_backrooms facade_hands, surf_minimumwage
  trackrider (ease); not to crt1/2, surf_ballzilla, kz_bhop_yonkoma, surf_tripportals (instant, user
  control). No path_track in the library has Disable Train (8), which is not modelled.
