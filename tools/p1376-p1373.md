## P1373: glare answers dropped on an eye jump

- Cause (older than P1363/P1364): Sprite_Glare traces only glares in the view cone, so an answer from the
  previous place survives while out of view; turned to after a teleport, the glare drew through the
  wall until the round-robin reached it, then faded over 50 ms.
- Change: a jump marks every traced answer stale (spr_gvis -1, ramp target 0) and zeroes the ramp; a
  stale glare is traced at the normal budget (centre blocked takes the one-ray shortcut). A jump is a
  move past vbsp_glare_jump (256) whose speed over clframetime beats 1.5x the last frame's + 1500 u/s,
  so hitches, low frame rates and 4x replays (steady speed) do not trip it; the frame after a jump is
  not tested, only records the new speed. vbsp_glare_jump 0 is the old behaviour.
- vbsp_glare_check 1/2: glares drawn while all five proxy rays are blocked, and the subset drawn on an
  answer traced before the last jump (the teleport case itself; the total is mostly 50 ms fade-outs).
- Arm (artifacts/run_glare_tele.py): surf_tensor2, stages 1-8 x 3 (24 setpos teleports), a full turn
  in 15-degree steps after each, A/B/B/A in one session. Final build (glare-tele-t2f.txt): jump 0:
  22,720 / 22,617 pre-jump glares drawn through walls (5,881 / 5,634 frames with one); jump 256: 0 / 0
  (total walled 522 / 0, the designed fade-outs). Every arm counted 24 jumps.
- Cost, check off (glare-tele-t2b-cost.txt): avg 708/635 (off) vs 692/667 (on) fps, 1% 244/194 vs
  240/238, 0.1% 104/96 vs 116/118 -- within the off arms' spread. A first version burst 4x rays after
  each jump: 1% low 225/239 -> 184/174, removed.
- Jump rule at 10 fps, the 4729 replay from 20 s (jumprule-*10.txt): five 300-unit resets -> 5 jumps in
  every build. A 4x stretch that starts from a pause: 13 jumps with the speed zeroed after a jump, 13
  with it kept, 3 with the frame after a jump untested (final); the same stretch at 1x: 2. Final build,
  1x -> 4x mid-play: 3; 4x play after a pause, twice: 3.
- Known limits (review 6): one blink on resuming a 4x replay below ~55 fps, a 1x -> 4x switch, a replay
  hitch over 0.5 s, a booster at 10 fps; a teleport on the frame right after another; in a 4x replay a
  300-unit teleport needs >75 fps at 3500 u/s; sv_gamespeed other than 1 lets cl_watch.qc's clock bank
  fire a false jump every 2nd-3rd frame in a 4x replay at low fps (FTESurf's slow practice uses
  host_timescale, which does not).
- Reviews: six rounds. Found and fixed: slow recovery for stale glares, jump test tripping on hitches
  and fast replays, migration adding MSAA to stepped-down configs, the engine-flag gate, CSQC frametime
  being the packet-stepped interpolation clock (now clframetime), chained jumps after a zeroed speed.
