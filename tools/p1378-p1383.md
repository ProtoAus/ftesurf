## P1378 -- passable tracktrains ride their path_tracks, in csprogs (QC)

Report: "entities that fly around the map simply don't move ... in the skybox". surf_centipedes' 3D sky:
105 skull props (boid{1,3}_anim.mdl) parented to 35 passable func_tracktrains (spawnflags 520) on one
306-node path_track loop at 60 u/s. func_tracktrain spawned frozen (Patch 257 / 860).

Owner steer, 8 Nov: "the server shouldn't really be doing anything with the movers" -- so:
- Server (sv_entities.qc): path_track's spawn keeps its keys (sh_trk.qc table) and still removes the edict
  (no renumbering). SV_TrkResolve (first frame, before the sky marks and the I/O build) walks each passable
  train's path (Find/Next/LookAhead: loops, rho shapes, dead ends, disabled next node, teleport node = a
  zero-length segment) and makes the train a CSQC entity (ENTWIRE_TRKSTATE: identity + render state once,
  clock s0/t0/speed/dir) with PVSF_IGNOREPVS. Non-solid prop_* whose parent is a riding train become
  ENTWIRE_TRKPROP (pose in the train's spawn frame + look, once). Inputs StartForward/StartBackward/Stop/
  Resume/Toggle/Reverse/SetSpeed/SetSpeedReal/SetSpeedDir(Accel)/TeleportToPathTrack re-anchor and resend
  the clock; any input to a train or rider resends its look; Kill removes riders; a dead end stops it.
  A train with anything drawn under it that cannot ride (a func_rotating wheel, a sprite, a trigger, a
  grandchild) stays frozen with it. Nothing moves an edict.
- csprogs (cl_trk.qc): path_tracks from the lump (getentitytoken), the same walk, trains and riders drawn
  every frame from `time` (the server clock). orientationtype 0 faces down the path once at s=0 (Find).
- Not modelled: per-path_track speeds (32 trains, 8 maps), OnPass/OnTeleport and train outputs, bank,
  velocitytype accel. surf_colony's sky canisters are solid 6 and loop by OnPass/OnTeleport: unchanged.

Measured (headless fteqwsv64 + FTEQuake client, isolated homedirs):
- Census (2,206 maps): 731 tracktrains, 438 passable on 51 maps, 369 moving at spawn, 403 on a loop, 65 with
  props in a 3D sky; inputs to passable trains: startforward 56, stop 31, setspeedreal 18, setspeed 12,

## P1380 -- point-sampled VTFs keep their mips; both clamp flags clamp (engine image.c, client)

centipede/centipedesign: VTF POINTSAMPLE|NOMIP|CLAMPS|CLAMPT, 1024x1024, the title ~986 texels wide drawn
~640 px at spawn. It wrapped (slivers of the next tile's "s" either side) and nearest sampling dropped
columns. Now CLAMPS+CLAMPT -> IF_CLAMP (one flag for both axes: a single-axis clamp keeps wrapping) and NOMIP
is not honoured on a POINTSAMPLE VTF (its own mip chain, GL_NEAREST_MIPMAP_LINEAR, nearest mag). P728's
Leidenfrost stays nearest up close. Verified: spawn screenshot beside the owner's -- slivers gone, even
strokes.
