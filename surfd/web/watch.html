Textures from your games

Maps download by themselves and bring most of their own textures. The rest come from your own Counter-Strike: Source, Half-Life 2 or Momentum Mod. Choose a game's folder once: what each map uses from it is kept in this browser, so later visits need no folder.

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Source

Paste the path into the window's address bar. Your browser will ask to "upload" it: this page only reads, on your computer, the files the map needs, and sends nothing. Add Half-Life 2 or Momentum Mod Playtest the same way. All of common also works, but the browser lists every file in it first (about 1½ minutes for 300,000 files).

Steam library on another drive? Use Chrome's or Edge's folder picker: it remembers the folder for next time (it cannot open folders under Program Files).
