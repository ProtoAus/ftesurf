Anti-cheat

Ranked runs are recorded and replayed on the server to verify movement and finish times.

Run verification

  1. Recording. During a run, the game records movement inputs, view angles, position and velocity. The recording also identifies the map build, timing zones and physics settings.
  2. Physics replay. A server re-simulates the recorded inputs using the same movement physics. It checks that the movement can be reproduced and that the finish time matches the map’s timing zones.
  3. Leaderboard result. The Verified badge marks a replay pass or developer approval after review. Runs can appear on the ranked board before they have this badge.

Boards and review

Ranked runs use enforced movement settings. Community times and imported Momentum Mod and KSF records are listed separately.

Recorded inputs are also analysed for signs of automation. The developer can review flagged or disputed runs and remove them from the board.

Ranked replays can be watched in game or on the web leaderboard.

Verification covers recorded movement and timing. A passing replay alone does not prove the absence of input assistance.

Report a problem

Send suspected cheats or security bugs privately to the developer on Discord.
